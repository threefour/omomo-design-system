# omomo Brand Identity

## Overview

omomo is a compact amplifier and full-range speaker designed for guitarists who want high-fidelity tone in a pedalboard-sized format. The brand reflects precision engineering, warmth, and intentionality.

## Core Values

- **Compact but powerful** — Small footprint, outsized impact
- **High fidelity** — Clean audio, no compromise
- **Handmade quality** — Thoughtful assembly, not mass-produced
- **Musician-centered** — Built by people who play

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette

**Primary**
- **Beige Red (RAL 3012)** — Warm, slightly aged, humanistic. Main enclosure color.
  - Hex: #C2685C (approximate)
  - Feels: Vintage, approachable, warm

**Secondary**
- **Matte Black** — Technical, neutral. Hardware accents, speaker cone.
- **Warm Gray** — Practical. Labels, secondary surfaces.
- **Gold/Brass** — Minimal accent. Handles (KIPP K0214), connectors. Premium feel.

**Neutral/Background**
- **Off-white / Natural** — Documentation, digital spaces.
- **Deep charcoal** — Text, high contrast.

### Typography

- **Headers / Marketing** — Sans-serif, geometric. Modern but not trendy. (Reference: Neue Haas Grotesk, Aktiv Grotesk)
- **Body / Technical** — Clean, readable sans-serif. High legibility at small sizes. (Reference: Inter, IBM Plex Sans)
- **Accent / Details** — Monospace for specs, serial numbers, technical details.

### Photography & Mood

- **Lighting** — Warm, natural light. Studio clean, not sterile.
- **Surfaces** — Materials matter. Show the beige-red powder coat, speaker driver, Hammond enclosure texture.
- **Props** — Guitar, pedalboard, cables. Show context.
- **Tone** — Warm, slightly aged aesthetic. Not futuristic. Not retro-kitsch.

## Product Architecture

### omomo model 01
- **Enclosure** — Hammond 1590DE, powder-coated RAL 3012 Beige Red
- **Speaker Driver** — Visaton FR10 (full-range)
- **Amp Board** — TPA3116
- **Handle** — KIPP K0214 (black or brass)
- **Finish** — Matte powder coat, minimal graphics

### omomo model 02
- **Enclosure** — Hammond 1590DE or similar
- **Speaker Driver** — Dayton RS100-4 (higher output)
- **Amp Board** — TPA3118
- **Handle** — KIPP K0214
- **Finish** — Same as model 01

## Labeling & Typography

- **Minimal branding** — "omomo" on front, critical specs on rear
- **Sans-serif, small cap** — Professional, not decorative
- **Embossed or laser-etched** — Texture; resists wear

## Tone of Voice

- **Clear, not flowery** — "Full-range speaker with built-in amplifier" not "sonic poetry"
- **Technical but warm** — Specs matter; so does feel
- **Direct** — For musicians, by musicians

## Carbon Footprint / Sustainability

- Local assembly (Hamburg)
- Minimal packaging
- Powder coat durability (extends life)
- Reference carbon calculations in documentation
